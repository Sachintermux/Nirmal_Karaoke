# Nirmal Karaoke v21 — Low-Latency Karaoke Transport

- Replaced the previous 650–3500 ms receive buffer policy with a 60 ms healthy target.
- Uses modern `RTCRtpReceiver.jitterBufferTarget` on supported browsers for audio-specific jitter-buffer control.
- Falls back to LiveKit `RemoteTrack.setPlayoutDelay()` only on browsers without `jitterBufferTarget`.
- Adaptive targets: 60/100/160/260/450 ms, max 700 ms, raised only after actual network evidence or a stall.
- Slow recovery prevents oscillation; latency is reduced in 20 ms steps after sustained health.
- Changed the admin AudioEngine from `latencyHint: playback` to `latencyHint: interactive` to reduce source-side Web Audio latency.
- Changed the audience AudioContext to `latencyHint: interactive` for duet mic responsiveness.
- Preserved stereo, RED, and high audio RTP priority.
- Preserved audio-first camera shedding.
