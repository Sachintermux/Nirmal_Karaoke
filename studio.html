CALLS & MEETINGSUse this studio as your microphone
OFF

Send your effected voice and music to Zoom, Meet, Teams or Discord. Choose a virtual audio cable below, then pick that cable's output as the microphone in your call app. You keep hearing yourself on your normal headphones.

How to set this up (one time)
  1. Install a free virtual audio cable: Download VB-Cable (Windows) or BlackHole (Mac). Restart your computer after installing.
  2. Use Chrome or Edge. Press Detect devices and allow the microphone once so names appear.
  3. Here, choose CABLE Input (the cable) and press Send to call.
  4. In Zoom / Meet / Teams / Discord, set the Microphone to CABLE Output.
  5. Start your mic here, pick a voice effect, play music. The call now hears this studio.
  6. Wear headphones and keep this site's main Audio output on them, not on the cable.
