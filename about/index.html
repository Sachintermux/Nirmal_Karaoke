About Nirmal Karaoke

Nirmal Karaoke is an online karaoke player and real-time voice studio that runs in your browser. There is nothing to install: open the page, allow your microphone and start singing.

What it does

  • Karaoke player with synced lyrics. Upload your own backing tracks or search songs, build a queue, and follow lyrics that scroll in time.
  • Voice lab. 33 original voice effects (10 classics, 9 character voices and 14 effect voices) plus a custom voice you build yourself, switched live while you sing.
  • Auto-Tune. Pitch correction with retune speed, scale and key controls, a live pitch needle and a coach readout.
  • Pro vocal chain. Compressor, de-esser, reverb, echo, mic boost, noise suppression and level meters.
  • Soundboard with hotkeys. Eight built-in sounds plus your own uploads, each with its own volume and optional key.
  • Live stage. Go live with one click, share a link or QR code, and let viewers listen, chat and request songs. Invite singers to duet with you.
  • Streaming. Send your show to YouTube, Twitch, Facebook or a custom RTMP server.
  • Calls and meetings. On desktop Chrome or Edge, send your effected voice to a virtual audio cable so Zoom, Meet, Teams or Discord can use it as a microphone.

Original voices, honest use

The voice presets are original styles shaped by pitch, tone and effects. They are not designed to imitate, and are not named after, any real person. Please use voice effects honestly and never to pass yourself off as someone else.

What you need

A recent Chrome or Edge is recommended for the studio. Watching a live stage works in any modern phone or computer browser. Any microphone works; headphones help avoid echo. Call output needs desktop Chrome or Edge and a free virtual audio cable, because phones cannot expose a virtual microphone to other apps.
