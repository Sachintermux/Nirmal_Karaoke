CALLS & MEETINGS

Voice changer for Zoom, Meet, Teams and Discord

Browsers cannot create a microphone by themselves, so Nirmal Karaoke sends its output to a free virtual audio cable and your call app listens to that cable as a microphone.

  1. Install VB-Cable (Windows) or BlackHole (Mac).
  2. In the studio Mixer, choose CABLE Input under "Calls & meetings" and press Send to call.
  3. In Zoom, Meet, Teams or Discord, set the microphone to CABLE Output.

Desktop Chrome or Edge. Phones cannot expose a virtual microphone to other apps.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Only the call-output feature needs a free virtual audio cable on your computer.

Does the voice change in real time?

Yes. Voices, Auto-Tune and effects are processed live as you speak or sing, so you can switch presets mid-song. Use headphones to avoid hearing a feedback echo.

Can it make me sound like a specific celebrity?

No, and it is not meant to. The voices are original styles shaped by pitch, tone and effects. Impersonating real people raises personality-rights and honesty problems, so we do not offer it.

How do my friends join a live stage?

Share the link or QR code from the Go live window, or invite them from the Singers lobby. Viewers need no account; invited singers can duet with their own microphones.

Can I use it in Zoom, Meet or Discord?

Yes, on desktop Chrome or Edge with a virtual audio cable (VB-Cable on Windows, BlackHole on Mac). The studio sends its output to the cable and your call app uses it as a microphone.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes for the studio, voices, soundboard and watching or joining live stages. Phones cannot provide a virtual microphone to other apps, so call output is desktop-only.

Where are my sounds and settings stored?

On your device, in your browser. Clearing the site data in your browser removes them.

Something sounds wrong or laggy. What should I try?

Use headphones, close other heavy tabs, set noise suppression to Low, and press Reset all in the Mixer to return every setting to normal.
